# Chosen Direction — Technical Gallery

## Design Movement

**Swiss International Typographic Style reinterpreted as a contemporary digital product index.** The visual system uses precision, structure, restraint, and purposeful asymmetry rather than decorative spectacle.

## Core Principles

1. **Type does the heavy lifting.** Monumental headlines and precise utility labels establish hierarchy without relying on imagery.
2. **Every line earns its place.** Hairline borders, markers, and metadata organise content like a product archive.
3. **Accent is a signal, not a surface.** Lime appears as a live indicator, active state, or a small unit of emphasis; never as a dominant background.
4. **Asymmetry creates pace.** Wide left-anchored content, offset cards, and spans of negative space keep pages editorial and intentional.

## Color Philosophy

Near-black **#0E0E0E** creates the feel of an editing bay or product shell, allowing the off-white **#F7F7F5** typography to appear crisp and physical. The lime **#C6FF00** reads like an active system status light: high-intent, sparing, and unmistakably YRD.

## Layout Paradigm

The site is designed as a **serial product ledger** instead of a centered marketing page. A fixed-feeling header edge and vertical guide establish the x-axis; sections are composed in unequal spans, with content frequently beginning at the left rule and project previews stepping outward into wide open space.

## Signature Elements

- **Lime status dots and coordinate marks** that denote active work, availability, and navigation state.
- **Browser-index panels**: abstract interface fragments with understated chrome, fine rules, and compositional data blocks.
- **Archive labels**: uppercase mono metadata, catalogue numbering, and horizontal continuation lines.

## Interaction Philosophy

Motion should communicate response, not decorate. Links offset a few pixels and reveal an underline bar; project panels rise or tilt imperceptibly according to cursor position. The menu opens as an information panel rather than a generic overlay.

## Animation

Use two consistent motion languages only: a 480ms transform-and-opacity entrance for content groups, staggered by 55ms, and an 180ms hover response for controls/panels. The abstract hero object may drift at a near-imperceptible rate. Respect reduced-motion preferences by presenting all content immediately with no transforms.

## Typography System

**Space Grotesk** is the geometric display voice, set in uppercase selectively and at high contrast for major statements. **DM Mono** provides calibrated labels, metadata, and system details. Body copy uses Space Grotesk at a relaxed line-height, with no generic sans-serif substitutions.

## Brand Essence

**YRD. builds intelligent digital products for organisations that need clear thinking translated into working systems.**

**Personality:** Exacting, composed, forward-looking.

## Brand Voice

Headlines are concise, declarative, and occasionally challenging. CTAs are conversational but direct; microcopy describes a real next step rather than selling hype.

> “Ideas are cheap. I build what comes next.”

> “Tell me what you’re working on. I’ll tell you what I think.”

## Wordmark & Logo

The wordmark is a sharp, wide **YRD.** lockup with a deliberately oversized terminal dot in lime. The compact mark is an angular **YR.** monogram; the symbol is an abstract split chevron framing a vertical lime signal. The mark should remain legible at favicon scale and never depend on tiny lettering.

## Signature Brand Color

**YRD Signal Lime — #C6FF00**

## Style Decisions

- Subpage hero compositions use the product-ledger grid: a visible metadata rail, a left rule, unequal spans, and an explicit catalogue/status signal rather than a centered marketing layout.
- Future work appears as intentional **archive reservations** with record numbers and unavailable status, never as a `TBD` placeholder.
- The YRD. wordmark is treated as a sharp custom lockup with a skewed glyph group and oversized signal-lime terminal dot rather than plain small text.
- Large areas of negative space carry quiet system annotations, coordinates, and continuation rules so they feel engineered rather than merely empty.
